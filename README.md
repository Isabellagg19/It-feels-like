# It-feels-like
It feels like is an interactive and really fun website where you can explore different pages depending on the mood you’re feeling that day, from nostalgic vibes to being completely focused on studying, and find a playlist made by me specifically for each mood. I also created spaces where you can share your suggestions for every playlist, so together we can build an amazing music community and create the best playlists possible. You'll also find plenty of fun facts that match the vibe of each playlist. And that's not all! On the home page, you'll find a community wall where you can share anything you want about music, your favorite songs, or even study tips with the rest of the It Feels Like community.

I built this project using HTML for the structure, from creating the different pages to organizing every detail, JavaScript to bring it to life especially the fun facts feature and CSS to give it my own special touch through text effects, unique backgrounds for each page, and the dynamic background on the home page.

This was my first project integrating content from Spotify, as well as platforms like Padlet and Google Forms. I really enjoyed creating the playlists and forms and learning how to bring everything together into a single website. The result is something unique that I would genuinely use in my daily life to discover new music for studying, relaxing, or listening to on a Sunday morning.

I truly hope you enjoy exploring it as much as I enjoyed creating it.
